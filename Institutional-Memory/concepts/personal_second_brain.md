# Personal Second Brain

## Definition

A system for offloading cognitive load by capturing, organizing, and retrieving information digitally.

## How it works

Information is ingested from various sources, processed into atomic notes, and linked to other related concepts and entities.

## Why it matters

Reduces information overload and enables long-term knowledge retention and discovery.
